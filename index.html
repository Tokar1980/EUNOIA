About the Project

An integrated view of European governance

Delivered through five interdisciplinary units, EUNOIA connects political science, international relations, economics, sociology and language policy. Students examine foreign and security actorness, economic coordination, fiscal governance, social inclusion, multilingualism and crisis communication, with particular attention to lessons from Ukraine’s crisis-time governance and democratic resilience.

Rationale

Why EUNOIA?

The Module responds to the need for deeper interdisciplinary understanding of how the EU builds coherent governance amid geopolitical instability, hybrid threats, economic fragmentation, democratic pressures and disinformation. Rather than treating policy fields in isolation, EUNOIA shows how EU instruments interact to support resilience and sustainable governance.

Objectives

What the project will achieve

Teaching & curricular integration

Deliver high-quality interdisciplinary EU teaching and strengthen students’ knowledge, critical thinking and intercultural competence.

Transnational cooperation

Deepen academic cooperation between Virginia Tech and SUTE through co-teaching, expert exchange and student engagement.

Open knowledge

Create and disseminate adaptable OERs, an EU Teaching Toolkit and research- and policy-oriented outputs.

EU visibility & sustainability

Expand public understanding of the EU while embedding quality assurance and continuation beyond the funded period.
