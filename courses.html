EUNOIA / Courses & Learning Activities

Courses & Learning Activities

A five-unit interdisciplinary pathway connecting European security, economic governance, fiscal coherence, social inclusion and multilingual administration.

Teaching programme

Open each unit to view its aim and sessions.

Lead: Prof. Yannis Stivachtis

Unit aim: To analyse how the EU’s security, strategic alignment, and external partnerships form a coherent framework for action beyond its borders.

Sessions

  1. Introduction: the goals and purpose of EUNOIA
  2. Building Europe’s Integrated Foreign and Security Capacity
  3. European Strategic Alignment and Transatlantic Partnership
  4. Enlargement and the Deepening of Europe’s Regional Integration
  5. Europe’s Neighborhood as an Integrated Partnership Zone
  6. European Multilateral Coherence in a Shifting Global Order
  7. Public Engagement and the EU’s Global Presence Strategy

Lead: Dr. Dimitris Tsarouhas

Unit aim: To explore how the EU coordinates economic governance and crisis response while integrating diverse national economies into a resilient common framework.

Sessions

  1. European Economic Coordination and Crisis Response
  2. European Conditionality and the Integration of Periphery and Core
  3. Learning and Policy Transfer in the European Governance Model
  4. European Economic Governance in a Changing Global Order

Lead: Prof. Volodymyr Tokar

Unit aim: To investigate the structures that enable Europe to align fiscal policy, financial innovation, and macroeconomic safeguards for integrated prosperity.

Sessions

  1. European Fiscal Rules and the Architecture of Budgetary Coherence
  2. Institutional Investors and Europe’s Financial Governance Fabric
  3. Artificial Intelligence and Financial Innovation for European Resilience
  4. Economic Shock Absorbers and Social Buffers in the EU Framework

Lead: Prof. Volodymyr Tokar

Unit aim: To examine how the EU mainstreams gender, migration, and local partnership models into a socially cohesive and inclusive governance order.

Sessions

  1. Gender Equality as a Cornerstone of European Social Integration
  2. European Migration Policy and Social Cohesion Mechanisms
  3. Community Clusters and European Inclusive Governance
  4. European Social Partnerships and Local Integration Mechanisms

Lead: Dr. Natalia Teslenko

Unit aim: To reflect on how multilingual governance and narrative coherence strengthen the EU’s administrative capacity and legitimacy in crisis settings.

Sessions

  1. Multilingual Governance and the European Administrative Space
  2. Narrative Coherence and Crisis Communication in Europe

Pedagogical methods

Thematic lectures, case-based seminars, EU policy simulations and role-plays, student-led collaborative research, and digital/hybrid learning.

Learning outcomes

EU literacy, comparative policy analysis, critical media awareness, intercultural competence, civic and digital skills, communication and consensus-building.

Student opportunities

Joint projects, policy briefs, simulations, public EU dialogues, research collaboration and engagement with international scholars.
